# Contract Risk Analyzer

## 1. Purpose & Trigger Conditions
Evaluates commercial agreements across 17 standard clause categories, benchmarks against market heuristics or playbooks, and scores exposure severity relative to the requesting party's role.

## 2. Severity Rubric
- **Critical**: Unbounded liability, unmitigated IP loss, or perpetual auto-renewal without exit.
- **High**: Material deviation from market standard with plausible financial/operational loss.
- **Medium**: Moderate deviation from standard positions, bounded or mitigable.
- **Low / Informational**: Minor deviations or standard boilerplate.

## 3. Mandatory Disclaimer
This report is an automated risk-flagging aid generated by Lumina and does not constitute legal advice. It may not capture every risk, may misclassify ambiguous language, and does not account for facts outside the reviewed document(s). Consult qualified legal counsel before making decisions based on this report.
